Privacy Policy: Choosing Hour Uploader

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Choosing Hour Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of The Choosing Hour YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it uses

It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own channel, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, and read that channel's own video statistics. It does not access any other person's account or data.

What it stores

A sign-in permission for the owner's account is kept privately in the owner's own settings. A log of uploaded video titles and IDs is kept. Nothing is sold or shared.

Google and YouTube

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the contact options on the channel.
